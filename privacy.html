PRIVACY POLICY
Packshoter

WHAT THIS COVERS

Packshoter is an internal production tool for 3D modelers. It is not a public service and is distributed only to contracted modelers. This policy describes what happens to personal data when a modeler signs in to Packshoter with a Google account.

WHAT WE RECEIVE

When you sign in with Google, we receive only:

  • your name
  • your email address

Nothing else. Packshoter does not request and cannot access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google service.

WHY WE NEED IT

Your email address is used for one purpose: to check that you are on the list of registered modelers and to determine what you are allowed to upload. Without it the tool cannot tell an authorized modeler from anyone else.

Your name is used only to show who is signed in, inside the application.

WHAT IS STORED, AND WHERE

  • On your own computer: a sign-in token, so you do not have to authorize the application every time. It is stored encrypted, under your Windows user account, and never leaves your machine except to renew the session with Google.
  • On our side: your name, email address and role, as part of the internal list of modelers. Records of uploads you make are linked to that entry.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it with third parties.
  • We do not use it for advertising or analytics.
  • We do not read any of your Google content.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Your entry is kept while you work with us. When cooperation ends, the entry is removed from the list of modelers. Records of work already delivered are kept as part of production history.

HOW TO REVOKE ACCESS

You can withdraw Packshoter's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that Packshoter will ask you to sign in again, and uploading will not be possible until you do.

To have your entry removed from the list of modelers, contact us at the address below.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy or about your data: kofein3d@gmail.com

This policy applies to the Packshoter desktop application only.
